Quantum measurement identifiability / 量子测量可识别性

Which additional Pauli correlations distinguish local rotations that look identical in same-axis measurements?

Measurement Aliasing and Pauli-Correlator Identifiability for Local Unitary Rotations of a Maximally Entangled Qubit Pair | 10.5281/zenodo.22840470

Research terms: Pauli correlators; quantum identifiability; measurement aliasing; local unitary reconstruction; finite-shot inference

Independent review request: Independent algebra checks and fair-budget reconstruction tests with abstention near degeneracy and held-out model checks.

Restricted-model identifiability proposal, not a new general tomography bound or evidence for hidden physical states.
这是限定模型的可识别性研究，不是普适层析新界限，也不是额外隐藏物理状态的证据。
