Pre-Projection Admissible State Spaces for Relational Rotation: A Structural Note on Flow, Coarse-Graining, and Finite Observational Readout

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-09-01 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22228060

Other public research records

Abstract

This structural preprint freezes an intermediate layer in a downward research decomposition. It separates an admissible pre-projection state structure, dynamical flow, observation/coarse-graining maps, and finite readouts. A planar relational-rotation benchmark shows that finite binary, eight-class, or twelve-sector readouts need not be identified with the dimension or ontology of the admissible state space. Common absolute orientation cancels from purely relational angle observables. The note explicitly does not claim a hidden-variable model, a new quantum state space, or a final ontology. It defines transition-relation reconstruction and discrete-versus-continuous symmetry as the next death gates.
