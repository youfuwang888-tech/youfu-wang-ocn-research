Resource-Loss Spectroscopy of Hidden Quantum Symmetries: A Rational Resolution Limit and a Minimal Operational Remedy

Wang, Youfu

2026-08-16 | Preprint | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21974356

Abstract / source description

We study reversible finite-group quantum instruments whose classical outcome record is coarse-grained over subgroups. The interactive-instrument resource loss is shown to equal a subgroup average of character intensity and to admit a direct Bell-return estimator. The resulting spectroscopy inherits a rational/cyclic-subgroup resolution limit. For a fixed labeled control oracle, two inequivalent three-dimensional representations of A5 are indistinguishable by every subgroup-resource measurement; they are exchanged by an outer automorphism, so the label-free gauge boundary is stated explicitly. A single class-sensitive five-cycle probe resolves the labeled ambiguity. A general rank-completion criterion identifies the minimum targeted probes needed to remove subgroup-incidence blind directions. The archive includes the manuscript, Supplemental Material, source files, code, frozen outputs, and finite-shot simulations. No hardware experiment is claimed.
