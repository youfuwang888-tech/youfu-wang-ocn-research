From a Three-Condition Pre-Generative Structure to Eight Quantum Processes, Hidden Binary Structure, and Binary Observation: The Yuan-Eight-Binary Unified Physical Hypothesis

Wang, Youfu

2026-09-02 | Publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22248366

Abstract / source description

This preprint proposes a strong-form, falsifiable four-layer physical hypothesis in which ordinary binary outcomes are coarse-grained projections of deeper physical organization. The Yuan pre-generative layer consists of three jointly necessary conditions: a carrier/possibility domain, boundary and constraint conditions, and internal coupling/organizational relations. Their joint realization is hypothesized to generate eight physically distinguishable quantum process sectors. In the strong form, these sectors are organized as four relational directions and two orientations with a D4-type composition structure. The generative layer projects into a hidden-binary structural layer containing ordered complementary pairs 01/10, A/B/C/D source or directional labels, an independent orientation attribute, and a 3-to-6-to-96 fine-grained relational record space, before conventional measurement coarse-grains the structure to 0/1. The hypothesis is deliberately frozen before experimental validation. It predicts recoverable hidden information beneath identical binary outcomes, a minimal eight-process description, D4 noncommutative composition fingerprints, resolvable fine-grained relational records, necessity of the three Yuan conditions, and cross-platform consistency. The next stage is a preregistered experimental program designed to support, modify, or falsify each layer independently.
