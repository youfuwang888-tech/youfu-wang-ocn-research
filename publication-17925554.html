Hidden-Phase Materials: A Six-Role Functional Classification Beyond the Periodic Table

Wangyoufu

Publication date: 2025-12-14 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17925554

Abstract

This work proposes a functional classification framework for materials capable of supporting or interacting with hidden quantum phases. Unlike conventional classification schemes based on elemental composition or energetic states, this framework categorizes materials according to the functional roles they may assume in stabilizing, triggering, coupling, dissipating, storing, or shielding hidden-phase behaviors. The six proposed roles&mdash;Phase-Shell, Phase-Trigger, Phase-Bridge, Phase-Dissipator, Phase-Memory, and Phase-Shield&mdash;are defined independently of specific chemical composition. The framework does not claim the discovery of new elements or materials, but instead provides a conceptual and structural language for interpreting anomalous or non-equilibrium material behavior observed in extreme or strongly constrained physical systems. By focusing on functional roles rather than substance identification, this classification aims to guide future experimental exploration and theoretical interpretation of hidden-phase phenomena, while remaining compatible with existing material science and quantum physics frameworks.
