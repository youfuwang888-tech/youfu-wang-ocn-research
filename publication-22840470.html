Measurement Aliasing and Pauli-Correlator Identifiability for Local Unitary Rotations of a Maximally Entangled Qubit Pair

Wang, Youfu

2026-09-19 | Preprint / author manuscript; not a journal publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22840470

Abstract / source description

We consider a restricted two-qubit model in which an unknown local unitary acts on one subsystem of a maximally entangled qubit pair and only local Pauli-product correlations are measured. We show that the three same-axis correlators XX, YY, and ZZ determine the magnitudes of the four components of the associated unit quaternion but, at generic interior points, leave an eightfold ambiguity in the underlying local rotation. We express this ambiguity as a sign fiber isomorphic to F2^3. The six cross-axis Pauli correlators separate into three rank-two sign-character subspaces. This structure yields a complete classification of the fifteen possible pairs of cross-axis correlators: the three reciprocal pairs XY/YX, XZ/ZX, and YZ/ZY remain rank deficient and generically leave a twofold ambiguity, whereas the remaining twelve pairs generically resolve the eightfold alias under explicit amplitude-nondegeneracy conditions. We then formulate a finite-shot adaptive reconstruction procedure that selects well-separated correlator groups and abstains near degeneracy, and we distinguish conditional reconstruction from held-out model auditing. The results concern identifiability within a restricted local-unitary model and do not constitute a new general tomography bound or evidence for additional physical states.
