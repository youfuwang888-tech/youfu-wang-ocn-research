On the Necessary Existence of Hidden-Phase Quantum Materials and Hidden Elemental States

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-12-14 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17925439

Quantum foundations and binary observationComplex systems, fields, materials, and stability

Abstract

This paper establishes the necessary existence of hidden-phase quantum materials and hidden elemental states based on quantum state completeness and cross-scale structural closure. Rather than identifying specific substances, it defines a new theoretical object and provides explicit derivation directions and falsifiable criteria (HPC-6) to guide future experimental discovery.
