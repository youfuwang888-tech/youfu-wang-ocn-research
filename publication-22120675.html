A Falsifiable Closure Hypothesis Beneath Binary Quantum Readout: Two-Face Pairing, Eight Configurations, and Staged Experimental Protocols

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-08-27 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22120675

Abstract

This conservative preprint formalizes a candidate directional closure structure beneath ordinary binary quantum readout. A handwritten sixteen-face table is transcribed into eight complementary closure pairs: at each of four ordered slots, the paired faces preserve the direction label and complement the binary value. The accompanying inversion audit shows that a single value-plus-direction label, such as 0_A, does not uniquely reconstruct a closure pair; a second independent readout or a frozen dynamical rule is required. The paper proves the standard same-state indistinguishability boundary: if distinct preparation histories yield the same density operator and all subsequent operations are path-independent completely positive trace-preserving maps and POVM measurements, no path-dependent signal can occur. It then defines three staged experimental protocols and their conventional adversaries, including process memory, leakage, state-preparation-and-measurement error, and drift. This record is an openly testable hypothesis and experimental protocol. It does not claim observation of sub-quantum constituents, discovery of new quantum states, or a replacement for standard quantum mechanics. ```
