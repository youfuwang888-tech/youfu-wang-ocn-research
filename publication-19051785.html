Defect–Fiber Spectral Geometry and Cosmological Freeze-Out: A Unified Framework for Higgs, Flavor, and Neutrino Structure

王有福

Publication date: 2026-03-16 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19051785

Abstract

This work presents a defect-fiber unified framework in which the Higgs sector, flavor hierarchies, neutrino suppression, and cosmological freeze-out are interpreted as low-energy readouts of a discrete spectral structure supported on cosmological defects. &nbsp; The framework is built from a temperature-dependent order-parameter field generating defects whose worldvolume carries an internal asymmetric fiber. The internal fiber supports a discrete spectrum whose lowest visible weak-doublet mode naturally corresponds to the Higgs sector, while higher modes organize flavor hierarchies. &nbsp; A unified bridge kernel defined along the internal fiber generates fermion mass matrices, mixing structures, and CP phases across quark, charged-lepton, and neutrino sectors. The framework also introduces a cosmological freeze-out chain in which the Standard-Model-like structure emerges as a residual low-energy readout after defect formation, internal fiber stabilization, internal splitting, and Higgs softening. &nbsp; Within this construction, Higgs physics, flavor organization, neutrino suppression, and cosmological phase evolution are treated as different manifestations of a single defect-supported spectral geometry. The model provides an executable toy pipeline linking cosmological parameters to low-energy observables including electroweak masses, CKM mixing, PMNS mixing, and CP hierarchy. &nbsp; Although not yet a complete fundamental theory, the framework defines a constrained and testable route toward a unified geometric organization of Higgs, flavor, neutrino, and cosmological freeze-out physics.
