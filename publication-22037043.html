DCR Open Validation Request: Three-Channel Cross-Intervention Model Separation

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-08-21 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22037043

Other public research records

Abstract

An open preregistration and reproducibility package for testing whether a constrained three-reservoir exchange model predicts a held-out recovery intervention better than registered standard competing models. The package contains the preregistered three-channel protocol, data dictionary, statistical analysis plan, competing-model definitions, reproducibility code, automated tests, and file-integrity checks. This package does not claim new particles, sub-quantum constituents, or physics beyond standard quantum theory. Its purpose is to enable an independent, falsifiable test of whether a mechanism-sensitive third observation channel improves cross-intervention model separation relative to registered standard alternatives.
