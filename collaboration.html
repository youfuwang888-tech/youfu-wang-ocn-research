Open Validation and Research Collaboration

Youfu Wang / 王有福 | OCN Research | ORCID 0009-0005-5998-6024

Purpose: find technically matched researchers and laboratories for independent replication, variable mapping, public-data replay, baseline correction, and scoped experimental review. Negative results and boundary corrections are explicitly welcome.

Current public test windows

  1. Quantum readout and closure hypotheses. Operational comparison of source-labelled or complementary-closure predictions against standard quantum models. Framework · staged protocol · formal boundary.
  2. Slow-light and photonic usable windows. Independent replay of protocol-dependent optimality and platform-integral window formation. slow-light prediction · protocol dependence · spectral reconstruction.
  3. Runtime structural governance. Evaluation of structure-constrained behavior and advisory-first runtime safety under failure-preserving replay. guardian layer · generative control.
  4. Three-channel model separation. Public request for cross-intervention evidence that can distinguish competing decoherence or residual-channel explanations. validation request.

What we can provide

  • bounded hypotheses, candidate variables, and explicit claim limits;
  • simulation scaffolds, replay protocols, baseline definitions, and failure tables where available;
  • non-confidential one-page concept notes and tailored validation cards;
  • co-authorship discussion only after a real shared contribution and agreed evidence standard.

What we are seeking

  • public or shareable experimental time series;
  • laboratory feasibility review and variable mapping;
  • independent replication, negative-result reporting, or baseline correction;
  • qualified teams interested in a small first test rather than endorsement of a large theory.
