AI-Driven Mining of Traditional Chinese Medicine Classics for Discovery of Next-Generation Artemisinin-Class Natural Medicines

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-03-12 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18972509

Other public research records

Abstract

This research proposes an innovative drug discovery route: TCM ancient books &rarr; AI pattern analysis &rarr; potent drug clues &rarr; modern experimental verification. Based on the core signals of &ldquo;single herb, remarkable efficacy, fresh use, low-temperature extraction&rdquo; in ancient medical classics, we screened out three high-value medicinal plants: Pulsatilla chinensis, Paris polyphylla, and Hedyotis diffusa. AI virtual verification confirmed that all of them contain thermolabile active ingredients with significant antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and anti-tumor activities. This study proves that TCM classics are a strategic treasure trove for global innovative drug research and development.
