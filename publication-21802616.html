Exact Sign-Sector Probabilities for the Diagonal of a Haar-Random Rotation in SO(3)

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-08-05 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21802616

Abstract

Let R be a Haar-random rotation matrix in SO(3). We determine the exact probabilities of the eight sign sectors defined by the diagonal entries (R_{11},R_{22},R_{33}). Each sign sector containing an even number of negative diagonal entries has probability 5/24, while each sector containing an odd number of negative diagonal entries has probability 1/24. Consequently, the total probability of the four even-parity sectors is 5/6, and the total probability of the four odd-parity sectors is 1/6. Two independent analytic derivations are presented. The first uses the unit-quaternion representation of Haar-random rotations together with Hopf-coordinate integration. The second uses independent Gaussian squares and the associated Dirichlet distribution with parameters (1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2). High-precision numerical integration and Monte Carlo simulation are included as independent checks of the exact formulas. The structural description of rotation-matrix diagonals, the quaternion representation of SO(3), and the relevant Haar and Dirichlet distributions are classical. The contribution of this work is the explicit evaluation of the eight diagonal sign-sector probabilities. We did not locate these explicit values in the sources searched.
