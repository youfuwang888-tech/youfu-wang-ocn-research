DCR-MRRS Hidden Risk Detector: A Three-Reservoir and Multiresolution Residual Framework for Early Risk Detection in AI Runtime Systems

Wang, Youfu; Wang, Yuhan

2026-06-26 | Preprint | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21016153

Abstract / source description

This preprint proposes the DCR-MRRS Hidden Risk Detector, a strong engineering-hypothesis module for AI runtime safety. The method combines the DCR-8/6 three-reservoir redistribution model with the MRRS/RLM-2^n multiresolution residual spectrum. It decomposes runtime risk into three reservoirs: A_visible for explicit behavioral or safety-rule risk, H_hidden for latent drift and accumulated hidden residuals, and R_env for environmental or historical-record risk. Unlike conventional safety layers that mainly detect collisions, boundary violations, or triggered rules, DCR-MRRS aims to detect cases where the system remains visibly normal while internal trends continue to deteriorate. The paper describes the input schema, reservoir scoring formulas, the DCR_total aggregate score, the DCR_phase_score decision score, the Hidden Trend Gate, and four minimal simulated test groups. The paper-based simulation indicates that the method remains normal in the baseline group, identifies explicit danger via A_visible, detects hidden drift through H_hidden even when the conventional safety layer does not trigger, and separates environmental disturbance through R_env. This work does not claim real-system validation. It is presented as a validation-ready method proposal for the Replay, Shadow, and Advisory layers of AI18-DIOS.
