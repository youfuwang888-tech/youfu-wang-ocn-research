Closure-Deficit Induced Protocol-Sensitive Decoherence in Open Quantum Systems

Wang, Youfu

2026-02-28 | Publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18810559

Abstract / source description

We propose a conservative structural extension to open quantum system dynamics in which decoherence includes a closure-deficit contribution governed by a completion parameter C ∈ [0,1], with ε = 1 − C representing structural deficit. The correction is formulated in Lindblad-compatible form, preserving complete positivity and trace conservation. It vanishes in the completion limit (C → 1), ensuring exact recovery of standard quantum mechanics. The model predicts a protocol-dependent decoherence signature under fixed environmental parameters, providing a falsifiable distinction from purely environment-driven decoherence models. This framework does not modify the core postulates of quantum mechanics but introduces a structurally constrained correction term.
