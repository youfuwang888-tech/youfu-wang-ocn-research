Structure-Constrained Behavior Generation: Minimal Evidence for Structure-Dominant Behavioral Dynamics

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-03-25 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19221403

Runtime governance and structure-constrained behavior

Abstract

We propose a minimal framework for behavior generation in dynamical systems, termed Structure-Constrained Behavior Generation (SCBG). &nbsp; Unlike traditional control paradigms where behavior is derived from optimization or learned policies, SCBG suggests that behavior emerges as a selection from a structure-constrained set of reachable modes. &nbsp; We construct a minimal 2D agent model with fixed local update rules and compare multiple structural configurations. Experimental results show that behavioral outcomes, success rates, and stability metrics are strongly dependent on structural configuration, even under identical update rules and inputs. &nbsp; Further perturbation studies indicate that structural modifications lead to significantly larger behavioral degradation than perturbations in input mappings, suggesting that structure acts as a primary determinant of behavior generation, while input plays a secondary, modulating role. &nbsp; This work presents a minimal evidence chain supporting the hypothesis that behavior can be generated through structural constraints rather than direct policy optimization.
