Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-11-07 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17552937

Quantum foundations and binary observation

Abstract

This collection brings together the core papers of Quantum Entanglement Mechanics (QEM) by Youfu Wang, covering the theoretical framework from QEM-4 to QEM-9. &nbsp; The QEM Series provides a unified description of entanglement propagation, topological interference, and non-local feedback. &nbsp; This Collected Works serves as the global entry point for citation, visibility, and long-term preservation under CC BY 4.0 license.
