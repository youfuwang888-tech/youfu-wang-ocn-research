QEM Zero–Twelve Dimensional Dual-State Cosmological Framework

Wangyoufu

Publication date: 2025-12-02 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17784670

Abstract

This work proposes the &ldquo;Zero&ndash;Twelve Dimensional Dual-State Cosmological Framework,&rdquo; a model in which the zero-dimensional origin and the twelve-dimensional maximal structure are mathematically identical through a negative-complex dimensional folding. This framework explains why the smallest observable scale and the largest cosmic scale converge to the same structural identity, forming a unified dual-state universe. It further introduces the AP-Hierarchy (active&ndash;passive hierarchy), observer-flip mechanism, and self-observation cosmology as key drivers of dimensional collapse and universal symmetry.
