A Closure–Sector Spectral Model for Quantum Correlation: A Local Closure Kernel Explanation of Entanglement

王有福

Publication date: 2026-02-26 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18786326

Abstract

This work proposes a Closure&ndash;Sector Spectral Model (CSSM) as a structured geometric framework for describing quantum correlations. The model introduces a 24-sector closure structure (VM-24) and a local closure kernel formalism to derive cosine-type correlation functions consistent with quantum entanglement statistics. &nbsp; The theory demonstrates: &nbsp; Cancellation of odd harmonics via half-cycle pairing symmetry Preservation of the no-signalling condition Recovery of the Tsirelson bound under bounded sector amplitude &nbsp; &nbsp; The framework is presented as a mathematical spectral model and does not claim modification of standard quantum mechanics, but offers a geometric closure interpretation of nonlocal correlations.
