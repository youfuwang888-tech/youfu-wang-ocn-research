A Forward Hypothesis of Recursive Closed Internal Structure Beneath Observable Binary States

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-07-22 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21482075

Quantum foundations and binary observation

Abstract

本文提出一种待验证的正向物理假设:外显二元状态0和1可能并非最终基本状态,而分别由方向相反的内部闭合关系01与10所生成。这里的01和10不被视为普通二进制字符串,而被视为具有顺序、方向、反演与闭合性质的不可约关系单元。 本文在暂时假定该假设成立的前提下,推导其可能产生的直接后果,包括同一外显状态下的内部差异、路径历史保留、内部反演、特征频率以及0/1镜像结构。进一步提出三种候选物理实现:方向&mdash;相位模型、概率流模型和闭合能量模型,并给出相应的正向实验验证路线,包括多路径相位实验、低功率弱谱学、内部反演搜索以及多类观测的联合检验。 本文不声称该假设已经获得实验确认,也不将三种候选模型视为最终物理结论。其目的在于把源于手稿的结构直觉转换为一个可计算、可证伪、可独立复现并可交由实验团队验证的研究框架,同时公开邀请具备量子控制、谱学、弱测量和多态读出能力的研究团队开展独立验证。 英文描述 This work proposes a falsifiable forward hypothesis that observable binary states 0 and 1 may not be fundamental, but may instead arise from oppositely oriented internal closed relations denoted as 01 and 10. These internal relations are not treated as ordinary binary strings, but as irreducible relational units carrying order, direction, inversion, and closure. Assuming the hypothesis is correct, the paper derives several possible consequences, including internal distinctions beneath the same observable state, path-history retention, internal inversion, characteristic frequencies, and mirrored structures between observable 0 and 1. Three candidate physical realizations are introduced: a directional-phase model, a probability-flow model, and a closure-energy model. Corresponding positive-direction experimental routes are proposed, including multipath phase tests, low-power spectroscopy, internal inversion searches, and joint analysis across multiple observables. No experimental confirmation is claimed, and the three models are not presented as final physical explanations. The purpose of this work is to convert a structural intuition originating from the author&rsquo;s manuscript into a calculable, falsifiable, independently testable research framework, and to invite experimental teams with capabilities in quantum control, spectroscopy, weak measurement, and multilevel readout to conduct independent validation.
