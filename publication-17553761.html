QEM-10: Control Theory and Testable Signatures of Non-Local Feedback in Quantum Entanglement Systems

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-11-07 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17553761

Quantum foundations and binary observation

Abstract

Abstract. We develop a control-theoretic framework of quantum entanglement built on an Oppositional Closure Network (OCN). When a local closure cannot be satisfied, the system performs an Instant Group Flip (I-GFlip) between the only two valid closures of a four-point unit (AB|CD and AD|BC) with no intermediate state. Under the Closure-Consistency Rule (CCR)&mdash;a shared edge that would break closure must flip&mdash;the flip cascades across an Entangled Square Lattice (ESL) as a Flip-Wave (FW). The framework preserves standard quantum-mechanical statistics and is compatible with no-signaling. &nbsp; Contributions. (1) A unified structural model: OCN + I-GFlip + CCR + FW on ESL. (2) Six testable signatures (S1&ndash;S6) of non-local feedback and structural re-closure. (3) A closed-loop control law that suppresses instability (e.g., reduces CHSH-variance). (4) Two prototype routes: Silicon Photonic PoC-&alpha; and a pre-synchronization pulse for quantum gates. &nbsp; Testable signatures (S1&ndash;S6). &nbsp; S1 Gain cap: visibility/correlation vs. gain shows a hump; low-frequency self-excitation appears near the peak. S2 Distance plateau: fidelity/CHSH vs. distance exhibits a near-flat window [L_\downarrow, L_\uparrow] with sharp decay outside. S3 Threshold knee N^*: a knee and mild hysteresis when sweeping injected noise. S4 Micro-asymmetry boost: tiny inter-layer phase mismatch (&asymp;0.5&deg;&ndash;3&deg;) increases stability before degrading. S5 Texture-lock sublinearity: phase-noise transfer becomes sublinear in textured/topological waveguides. S6 Invisible routing: keep total path length fixed; reshape peaks by only changing arm weights (center fixed, shape tunable). &nbsp; &nbsp; Control law (discrete, summary). Let y_t be a target observable (e.g., CHSH S or visibility) and D_t the closure-debt (count of non-closing shared edges). The controller updates a phase/gain command u_t via u_t = k_p e_t + k_d(e_t - e_{t-1}) - k_c D_t, steering the network toward CCR-satisfying configurations and reducing instability. &nbsp; Reproducibility. This record includes: &nbsp; QEM-10_Manuscript.pdf (main paper); QEM-10_Supplement_Code.txt (minimal ESL toy model pseudocode for I-GFlip + CCR + FW); Suggested parameters: O/B/T triggers (0/1), defect density p, steps T, gain g, delay \tau, distance L, noise power N. Block-swap maps (AB|CD &harr; AD|BC), FW front shapes, and closure-debt trajectories can be reproduced from the toy model. &nbsp; &nbsp; Clarification (no-signaling). Here &ldquo;instantaneous&rdquo; means a binary structural re-closure (I-GFlip), not energy or information transmission; the framework respects the no-signaling principle. &nbsp; Keywords.
