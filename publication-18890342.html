Λ-field Single Scalar Field Framework for Structural Unified Field Modeling and Predictive Localized Modes in Slow-Light Systems

王有福

Publication date: 2026-03-06 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18890342

Abstract

This work presents a &Lambda;-field single scalar field framework for structural unified field modeling, aimed at describing localized compression phenomena in slow-light systems. The proposed model introduces a nonlinear scalar field with a cosh-type potential and derives the corresponding Euler&ndash;Lagrange equation governing system dynamics. Small oscillation analysis reveals a gapped dispersion spectrum, indicating stability of the field under small perturbations. A static one-dimensional formulation is further developed to predict localized compression modes and boundary-locked structures. An experimental comparison interface (NEC-1 first window) is proposed, allowing potential validation using representative slow-light datasets such as photonic crystal slow-light experiments. The framework establishes a predictive connection between theoretical field configurations and observable quantities such as group index and group velocity. This version (v1) presents the theoretical framework and predictive structure. Further empirical validation, including NEC-1 second-window analysis and Gate-A1 scaling tests, is reserved for future work. The present study provides a theoretical basis for exploring localized energy compression phenomena across photonic and related physical systems.
