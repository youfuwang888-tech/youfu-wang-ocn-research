QEM-8: Unified Equations for Hierarchical Mirror Lattices

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-11-07 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17547630

Other public research records

Abstract

Abstract (EN): We unify QEM-4&ndash;7 into a single field framework for hierarchical mirror lattices. Starting from a variational functional for &Sigma;/&Delta; modes with a domain-wall order parameter, we derive coarse-grained equations across layers, an RG-like flow for effective couplings, and a universal causal-cone bound that collapses to QEM-5 locally and to an array form in QEM-7. The coherence length scales as &xi; ~ |&theta;&minus;&theta;c|^{-1/2} at each level. We provide falsifiable predictions and cross-level protocols. Series note: This work is part of the Quantum Entanglement Mechanics (QEM) series by Youfu Wang (2025). 中文摘要 (ZH): 我们把 QEM-4&ndash;7 统一到层级镜像格的场论框架中:从变分泛函出发得到跨层粗粒化方程与&ldquo;类重整化&rdquo;的耦合流;统一的因果锥上限在局域退化为 QEM-5,在阵列退化为 QEM-7。相关长度在各层满足 &xi; ~ |&theta;&minus;&theta;c|^{-1/2};重整后的参数把约束向上层级传递,给出芯片级时序预算、扇出与稳定窗口。并给出跨层可检验流程与普适性指纹(&pi; 回声、sech&sup2; 包络、前沿速度塌缩律)。
