Temporal Correlations Reshape Causal Resources in Qubit Hamiltonian Recognition: Exact Two-Query General-Strategy Phase Diagram

Wang, Youfu

2026-09-10 | Preprint / author manuscript; not a journal publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22685037

Abstract / source description

Hamiltonian recognition asks which candidate Hamiltonian generated an unknown quantum evolution when the evolution angle is not observed. This preprint solves a binary two-query qubit setting in which the two unknown angles range from independently resampled to fully shared. A task-specific symmetrization theorem reduces the full sequential optimization exactly to a three-dimensional correlation elliptope. A nine-dimensional legal subset of the most general tester dual is then shown to reduce, by semidefinite-program duality, to the same elliptope problem, yielding P_GEN(beta,eta) = P_SEQ(beta,eta) throughout the stated parameter domain. The resulting PAR-versus-GEN phase diagram contains an exact interior equality curve and an exact high-angle reentrant regime in which the globally optimal causal-processing advantage vanishes at both zero and full temporal correlation but is positive at every intermediate strength. Arbitrarily weak temporal correlation can activate a strict advantage. In this archival v1.1 release, the weak-correlation statement is conservatively formulated as explicit constructive lower bounds of orders eta^2 below 45 degrees, eta^(4/3) at 45 degrees, and eta above 45 degrees. No matching upper-bound derivation is claimed for exact asymptotic equivalence of those exponents. The record includes the main preprint, Supplemental Material, and a reproducibility archive containing proof notes, symbolic verification scripts, numerical cross-check data, revised manuscript source, figures, and integrity hashes. Status: public preprint; not independently peer-reviewed.
