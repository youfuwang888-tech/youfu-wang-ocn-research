Non-Abelian Information Loss under Homomorphic Binary Coarse-Graining of D4: Fourier Reconstruction, Symmetric Dynamics, and Coherent Reference Recovery

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-08-29 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22152007

Quantum foundations and binary observation

Abstract

This preprint develops an exact mathematical and quantum-information framework for information loss under homomorphic binary coarse-graining of the eight-element dihedral group D4. It derives the D4&rarr;Z2&times;Z2 abelianization, an exact finite-group Fourier reconstruction using three one-dimensional character moments plus one 2&times;2 non-Abelian Fourier matrix, a two-rate symmetric D4 relaxation spectrum with the blind-prediction relation gamma_RS = gamma_R + gamma_S, an information budget for 8&rarr;4&rarr;3 coarse-graining and context-rich refinements, and a coherent-reference protocol in which the commutator residue -I is invisible as a target channel but observable as a relative phase. All results lie within standard probability theory, finite-group representation theory, and standard quantum mechanics; no beyond-standard quantum claim is made.
