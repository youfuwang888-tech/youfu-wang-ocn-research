QEM-8^3: Entanglement Square Lattice and Flip-Wave Dynamics

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-11-05 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17536955

Quantum foundations and binary observation

Abstract

This paper introduces QEM-8^3, a three-dimensional extension of the QEM-8 entanglement framework. The model treats each entangled pair (A&harr;B and C&harr;D) as both an individual binary relation and part of a larger lattice system. By stacking the eight directional entanglement modes along vertical, horizontal, and depth axes, the structure forms a stable entanglement square lattice. The transitions between AB|CD and AD|BC are explained through measurement sequence, basis switching, and stability reconfiguration. The result is a discrete algebraic lattice that preserves absolute duality and relational symmetry across multi-quantum entanglement systems.
