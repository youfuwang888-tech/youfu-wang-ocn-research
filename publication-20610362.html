The Structure of Fate: An AI-Human Co-Discovery of Reality, Choice, and Civilization

Wang, Youfu

2026-06-09 | Book | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20610362

Abstract / source description

This public abridged English edition presents the core arc of a longer Chinese manuscript on fate, structure, human-AI collaboration, complex-system governance, and future civilization. The work reframes fate not as superstition or fixed destiny, but as the felt pressure of structure: the way freedom encounters visible and invisible conditions. It explores how human intuition and AI-supported recursive reflection can jointly develop a broader language of structure, constraint, path opening, runtime governance, and shared responsibility. This edition is not a completed scientific theory or validation claim. It is a philosophical nonfiction essay and public conceptual artifact intended for open reading, international feedback, and future research dialogue.
