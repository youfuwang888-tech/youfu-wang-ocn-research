Outcome-Frame Monodromy and Exact Limits of Marginal-Only Quantum Feedback

Wang, Youfu

2026-09-12 | Preprint / author manuscript; not a journal publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22715996

Abstract / source description

A quantum instrument is not determined by its induced positive-operator-valued measure (POVM) and nonselective channel, yet the global control consequences of this residual branch structure remain incompletely characterized. We study the nondegenerate fiber of instruments with both marginals fixed. The subgroup of outcome permutations preserving the effect tuple acts as the deck group of the ordered outcome-frame covering, and closed loops define a conjugacy-class-valued monodromy representation. Nontrivial monodromy obstructs continuous global branch labeling based only on local process continuity. We construct an explicit cyclic family with a uniform m-outcome POVM and a fixed completely dephasing channel, derive robust overlap-based reconstruction and a conservative finite-sample guarantee, and certify entangled conditional Choi states although the nonselective channel is entanglement breaking. For a noisy cyclic family, we prove an exact minimax separation in conditional feedback: recovery rules restricted to the two marginals and the reported local outcome have optimal worst-sector entanglement fidelity 1/m, whereas an outcome-frame-aware controller attains 1-lambda+lambda/m. The exact minimax value of the missing outcome-frame information is therefore (1-lambda)(1-1/m). The results remain entirely within standard finite-dimensional quantum mechanics and are positioned as an instrument-level, permutation-valued specialization of established Kraus-frame/channel holonomy with a distinct global-labeling and feedback limitation.
