QEM-8 II: A Hadamard-Tetrahedral-Spinor Framework for Eight-Outcome Qubit Geometry, Coarse-Graining, and History-Sensitive Tests

Wang, Youfu

2026-09-21 | Preprint / author manuscript; not a journal publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22866412

Abstract / source description

This preprint develops a mathematically explicit QEM-8 framework for a single qubit. It separates eight-state Bloch geometry, spinor sign double covers, and quaternionic operation history; derives the three-bit cube and parity-tetrahedral SIC structure; connects V4, H4 tomography, SU(2)/Q8 spinor lifts, and an eight-outcome cube POVM at the eta=1/sqrt(3) joint-measurability boundary; decomposes eight-outcome statistics into Walsh modes 1+3+3+1; and defines a rigorous 8->6->8' projection through a six-outcome Pauli representation. The residual Delta=p-p' lies in the four-dimensional high-order Walsh sector. Standard projective history effects (Gate 1A) are explicitly separated from a stronger, falsifiable history-residual hypothesis (Gate 1B). No experimental violation of standard quantum mechanics is claimed.
