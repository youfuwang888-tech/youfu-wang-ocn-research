QEM-6: Mirror-Flip Quantum Unit as the Minimal Structural Cell of Entanglement

Wangyoufu

Publication date: 2025-11-07 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17547479

Abstract

Abstract (EN): We introduce the Mirror-Flip Unit (MFU) as the minimal structural cell that realizes and stabilizes multi-body entanglement within the QEM framework. The MFU is a dual-layer mirror lattice supporting &Sigma;/&Delta; collective modes with a controllable &Delta;-domain-wall profile that confines coherence. We derive governing equations near stationary textures, obtain the scaling &xi; ~ |&theta;&minus;&theta;c|^{-1/2}, and predict falsifiable signatures: &pi;-echo, sech^2 standing envelopes, and a causal front bounded by v_max &asymp; (J &xi;)/ħ. Protocols are provided for wall-fit (&xi;), front-tracking (v_max), and stability-window mapping.
