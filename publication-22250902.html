Yuan–Eight–Binary Unified Physical Hypothesis: Master Experimental Validation Protocol v1\.0

Wang, Youfu

2026-09-02 | Publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22250902

Abstract / source description

This protocol converts the strong-form Yuan–Eight–Binary Unified Physical Hypothesis into a staged, falsification-first experimental validation program. The theory baseline is Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22248366. The protocol separates four levels—Yuan pre-generative conditions, eight quantum/generative processes, hidden binary relational structure, and ordinary 0/1 observation—and defines E01–E11 experimental cards with explicit prerequisites, observables, PASS/FAIL rules, confound controls, and maximum permissible conclusions. It also introduces an S0–S6 evidence ladder so that a positive result at a lower layer cannot be used as proof of a higher layer. The protocol is platform-portable and is intended to be instantiated through device-specific preregistration annexes for quantum photonic, superconducting, trapped-ion/atomic, or other controllable quantum systems. The central unresolved quantitative task is the platform-specific bridge F(Ω,B,C)→G8, which must be frozen before the E10 bridge experiment is run.
