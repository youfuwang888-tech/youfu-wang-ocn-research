A Binary-Observable, Quaternary-Closure Hypothesis for Quantum Systems: Formal Model, Operational-Equivalence Boundary, Simulation Audit, and Open Experimental Tests

wangyoufy王有福

Publication date: 2026-07-21 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21465115

Abstract

本文提出一种可证伪的&ldquo;二元显现&mdash;四元闭合&rdquo;量子假设。该假设源于早期手稿中的结构直觉:量子系统在通常测量中表现为0或1,但其内部可能并非两个孤立状态,而是由绝对对立、同时出现并受反演关系约束的四元方向结构;外部二元读出可能是对该内部结构的粗粒化投影。 本文将这一假设形式化为四维候选状态空间: {|0,+&gt;, |0,&minus;&gt;, |1,+&gt;, |1,&minus;&gt;} 并定义二元粗粒化测量、反演配对与闭合关系。研究同时完成了多轮合成仿真、强竞争模型比较和操作等价性审查。 当前结果表明:只要该模型仍使用标准有限维量子态、量子通道和POVM测量,它就可以与具有相同状态、动力学和测量结构的普通四维量子模型操作等价。因此,本文不声称该假设已经超出标准量子力学,也不声称&ldquo;0内部存在01、1内部存在10&rdquo;已经获得实验验证。 本文的主要贡献包括: 恢复并形式化二元显现&mdash;四元闭合假设; 明确其与Bell态、双轨编码和普通四维量子系统的关系; 给出操作不可区分性结果和否定边界; 公开已完成的仿真、失败结果和强对手审查; 提出维度见证、正反路径制备、顺序测量、闭合循环及跨平台验证协议; 邀请量子基础、量子控制、超导量子比特、光子量子信息、离子阱、自旋系统和开放量子系统研究者进行独立验证或证伪。 真正有待实验回答的问题是:不同名义二能级量子系统是否会在没有人为加入辅助编码的情况下,自然呈现同构的四元闭合结构;或者是否存在普通多能级泄漏、环境记忆、几何相位、控制误差及标准量子过程无法解释的稳定统计规律。 本记录定位为假设论文、形式模型、否定性结果和公开验证请求。否定结果与支持结果具有同等科学价值。 English summary This record presents a falsifiable binary-observable, quaternary-closure hypothesis for quantum systems. The hypothesis proposes that systems producing ordinary binary outcomes, 0 and 1, may contain an internal four-position directional structure constrained by opposition and inversion, while standard measurements coarse-grain that structure into two visible outcomes. The candidate state space is formalized as: {|0,+&gt;, |0,&minus;&gt;, |1,+&gt;, |1,&minus;&gt;} The work includes synthetic simulations, strong competing-model comparisons, an operational-equivalence audit, falsification criteria, and proposed experimental protocols involving dimension witnesses, opposite preparation paths, sequential measurements, closure cycles, and cross-platform replication. The current mathematical form remains operationally equivalent to an ordinary symmetric four-dimensional quantum model when the same states, dynamics, and measurements are used. Therefore, no experimental confirmation and no claim beyond standard quantum mechanics are made. The open empirical question is whether nominal two-level systems naturally exhibit the same hidden closure symmetry across distinct physical platforms, or whether reproducible statistics arise that cannot be explained by multilevel leakage, environmental memory, geometric phase, control errors, or standard quantum-process descriptions. This record is released as a hypothesis paper, formal model, negative and boundary analysis, and an open request for independent experimental validation or falsification.
