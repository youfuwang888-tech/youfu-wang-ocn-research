Publication date: 2025-11-08 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17558618

Abstract

This paper extends the Quantum Entanglement Mechanics (QEM) series toward an integrated model of matter&ndash;mind&ndash;existence. It formalizes the Closed-Opposition Network (OCN&infin;) and the Consciousness&ndash;Quantum Coupling (CQC) mechanism, suggesting that consciousness acts as an internal trigger of entangled flip (I‑GFlip).
