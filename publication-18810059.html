Closure-Corrected Cosmology: Plateau and Inflection Signatures Beyond ΛCDM

王有福

Publication date: 2026-02-28 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18810059

Abstract

We investigate a closure-corrected extension of &Lambda;CDM cosmology in which deviations arise from a structural completion parameter C(a), with closure deficit &epsilon;(a) = 1 &minus; C(a). The effective dark energy density acquires a controlled dynamic component proportional to &epsilon;(a). A quasi-plateau regime naturally emerges when structural parameters satisfy &beta; &asymp; &gamma;, leading to mild but structured deviations from standard &Lambda;CDM behavior. This model preserves the General Relativity backbone and does not modify Einstein equations. It introduces a minimal phenomenological correction term with explicit recovery in the completion limit (C &rarr; 1). The framework provides: - A parameter-controlled deviation space - Explicit &Lambda;CDM recovery - Plateau and inflection signatures in late-time cosmology - A conservative extension without new propagating degrees of freedom This work does not claim to resolve the Hubble tension but provides a structurally motivated deviation framework that is observationally testable.
