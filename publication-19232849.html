A Structural Generative Control Framework: Behavior Mode Generation under Structural Constraints

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-03-26 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19232849

Runtime governance and structure-constrained behaviorComplex systems, fields, materials, and stability

Abstract

This work proposes a Structural Generative Control Framework (SGCF), a novel control paradigm in which control is formulated as behavior generation under structural constraints rather than direct action computation. Unlike traditional approaches such as PID, MPC, and reinforcement learning, which map system states directly to control actions, SGCF introduces an intermediate behavioral layer defined by a finite set of behavior modes. These modes emerge as stable solutions of an underlying structure composed of driving forces, relational constraints, and system limitations. The framework demonstrates clear advantages in critical scenarios including goal&ndash;obstacle conflicts, instability, and rapid behavior switching. In these cases, SGCF maintains system feasibility through continuous transitions between behavior modes, avoiding discontinuities and collapse observed in conventional methods. SGCF provides a unified formulation bridging structure, behavior, and control, and establishes a new perspective for organizing control systems beyond optimization- and rule-based approaches.
