Platform Integral Predicts Slow-Light Window Formation in Photonic Crystal Waveguides

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-05-05 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20039355

Photonics, slow light, and spectral windowsComplex systems, fields, materials, and stability

Abstract

Slow-light phenomena in photonic crystal waveguides are commonly characterized by large group index values near band edges. However, the usable slow-light window is not solely determined by the peak group index. &nbsp; In this work, we apply a platform integral framework to analyze slow-light window formation. Instead of relying on local extrema, we define a global measure based on the group velocity landscape: &nbsp; P^*_{slow} = \int \exp(-v_g/\langle v_g\rangle)\cdot \exp(-|\partial_\lambda v_g|/\langle|\partial_\lambda v_g|\rangle)\, d\lambda &nbsp; where v_g is the group velocity. Using published results on flatband slow-light modes in photonic crystal waveguides, we show that usable slow-light windows correspond to extended regions of low and slowly varying group velocity, rather than isolated extrema. &nbsp; This leads to a structural interpretation: the peak group index determines the slow-light center, while the integrated weight of low-velocity plateaus governs the usable window width. &nbsp; Together with prior polariton-based results, this supports a cross-system platform integral mechanism for window formation in hybrid mode systems.
