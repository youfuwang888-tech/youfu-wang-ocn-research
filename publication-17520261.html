QEM-8: A Direction-Based Collapse Algebra for Quantum Entanglement Modes

wang, youfu

2025-11-04 | Thesis | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17520261

Abstract / source description

This paper proposes QEM-8, a discrete directional-mode algebra explaining eight asymmetric quantum collapse modes. The framework provides mathematical insight into direction-conditioned entanglement and hidden causal asymmetry in quantum communication.
