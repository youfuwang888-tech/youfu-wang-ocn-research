QEM-8³: Hierarchical Mirror Lattice and Sequential Flip-Wave Dynamics in Multi-Body Entanglement

Wangyoufu

Publication date: 2025-11-06 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17538631

Abstract

We present QEM-8&sup3;, a hierarchical mirror-lattice framework for multi-body quantum entanglement. Non-commutativity of measurement order and basis rotation jointly induce Sequential Trigger Flips (STF), whose propagation forms Flip-Waves across nested mirror layers. We derive a coupled flip-wave equation, identify a Critical Layer (mirror&ndash;basis resonance), define the symmetry group G_{8^3} and a topological invariant T_{8^3}, and predict quasi-time-crystalline spectral oscillations, time-reversal echoes, and critical entanglement peaks.
