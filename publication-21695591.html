Recursive Binary Words as Dimension-Bounded Temporal Memory Witnesses

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2026-07-30 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21695591

Abstract

Reproducibility archive for exact deterministic memory complexity and certified equal-dimension classical-quantum temporal separations for finite Thue-Morse words under a stationary, clock-free generation model. The archive contains source code, exact and interval certificates, machine-readable outputs, figures, manuscript source, and the compiled manuscript. The results are trusted-dimension temporal-memory witnesses; they are not device-independent statements, do not establish hidden quantum states, and do not modify standard finite-dimensional quantum theory. Source code is released under the MIT License; manuscript materials are released under CC BY 4.0.
