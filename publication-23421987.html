Temporal Correlations Reshape Causal Resources in Qubit Hamiltonian Recognition: Exact Two-Query Strategy Comparison and a Uniform Critical Crossover

Wang, Youfu

2026-10-16 | Preprint / author manuscript; not a journal publication | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23421987

Abstract / source description

We investigate binary recognition of an unknown qubit Hamiltonian using two queries of its evolution, with unknown evolution angles that are either independently resampled or shared with tunable temporal correlation. For a symmetric pair of nonorthogonal qubit Hamiltonians, we compare parallel (PAR), sequential (SEQ), and general (GEN) quantum strategies. An exact symmetry reduction expresses the sequential optimization through a three-dimensional correlation elliptope. A general-strategy dual certificate establishes the equality between optimal general and sequential strategies within the studied model. We analyze the operational advantage generated by temporal correlations and establish distinct weak-correlation scaling regimes separated by a critical angle of 45 degrees. This revised and extended version (v1.2) adds a uniform double-scaling critical crossover theorem, including an explicit positive crossover function and consolidated mathematical proofs. The result characterizes a previously unresolved critical transition within the specified quantum Hamiltonian recognition model. The complete mathematical derivations are provided in the accompanying supplementary material (Sections S1–S19). This work is conducted within standard quantum information theory and does not claim physics beyond quantum mechanics.
