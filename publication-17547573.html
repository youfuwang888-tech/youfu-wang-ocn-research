QEM-7: Energy-Field Coupling and Feedback in Mirror-Flip Arrays

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-11-07 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17547573

Complex systems, fields, materials, and stability

Abstract

Abstract (EN): We extend the QEM-6 Mirror-Flip Unit (MFU) to device arrays and introduce an energy-field coupling model with readout&ndash;feedback control. Linearization around stationary domain-wall textures yields a Laplacian-spectrum-governed stability condition; we derive conservative bounds for feedback gain and delay, and generalize the flip-wave propagation limit to arrays. Three falsifiable protocols are provided: energy-flux pump&ndash;probe, feedback stability-island mapping, and array response spectroscopy. The results set timing budget, fan-out limits and safe operating windows for chip-oriented, controllable entanglement networks. Series note: This work is part of the Quantum Entanglement Mechanics (QEM) series by Youfu Wang (2025). 中文摘要 (ZH): 我们把 QEM-6 的最小镜像翻转单元(MFU)推广为阵列系统,提出带读出-反馈闭环的能量场耦合模型。在畴壁稳态附近进行线性化,得到由阵列拉普拉斯谱主导的稳定条件;导出反馈增益与延迟的保守临界,并将翻转波传播上限推广到阵列场景。提出三类可验证流程:能量通量泵浦-探测、反馈稳定岛扫描、阵列响应谱测量。结果为芯片级可控纠缠网络给出时序预算、扇出限制与安全工作区。系列说明:本作属于王有福(Youfu Wang)在 2025 年提出的 QEM 系列研究。
