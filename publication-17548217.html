QEM-9: Hyperstacked Mirror Lattices under the Opposition Principle (v2.1)

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-11-07 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17548217

Complex systems, fields, materials, and stability

Abstract

Abstract. We extend the Quantum Entanglement Mechanics (QEM) framework from 3D mirror lattices to d-dimensional (and asymptotically infinite-dimensional) hyper-stacked lattices by enforcing a &Sigma;/&Delta; Opposition Principle. Each stacking axis is assigned a Z₂ opposition operator O_k; the global opposition O^{(d)}=\bigotimes_k O_k confines admissible modes to the odd (&Delta;) sector. Within this sector we derive: (i) a sector-resolved upper bound on propagation speed, v_{\max}^{(d)} \approx \min\!\big(v_{\text{local}},~\tfrac{1}{\pi}\sqrt{a_\parallel^2\Omega_0^2+\sum_k a_k^2 c_k^2 \lambda_{\max,k}^{\text{odd}}}\big); (ii) stability islands with delay limits \tau_c(d)&lt;0.2\,\min_k\{\xi_k/c_k\}; and (iii) a coherence scaling \xi_d\sim\xi_1/\sqrt{d}. We show controllability as d\to\infty provided \sum_k c_k^2 is bounded. Engineering rules are given as a design-for-opposition SOP/DRC: bipartite layouts, loop-parity (even &Sigma; count), &pi;-inverters on odd-&Sigma; paths, and sector-selective validation (parity-echo, basis-flip, sector spectroscopy). The results unify QEM-4&hellip;7 and yield practical constraints for scalable quantum/photonic/atomic architectures.
