QEM-9: Hyperstacked Mirror Lattices under the Opposition Principle (v2.1)

Wang, Youfu

Publication date: 2025-11-07 | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17549464

Quantum foundations and binary observationComplex systems, fields, materials, and stability

Abstract

English We extend the Quantum Entanglement Mechanics (QEM) framework from 3D mirror lattices to d-dimensional hyperstacked lattices governed by a &Sigma;/&Delta; opposition principle. Transport and stabilization are computed in the odd (&Delta;) sector, yielding sector-resolved propagation bounds, stability-island delay limits, and a coherence scaling \xi_d \approx \xi_1/\sqrt{d} when \sum c_k^2 is bounded. We also summarize a design-for-opposition SOP for scalable architectures (bipartite layout, loop-parity constraints, and &pi;-inverters on odd-&Sigma; paths). Series: Quantum Entanglement Mechanics (QEM), 2025. License: CC BY 4.0.
